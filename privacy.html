PowerBlock

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

PowerBlock does not collect, sell, share or transmit your personal data or browsing data. Everything it does happens inside your browser, on your device.

Who we are

PowerBlock ("the extension") is a browser extension that blocks ads, trackers, pop-ups and other unwanted page content. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how.

Information handled on your device

To block ads, the extension has to look at the pages you visit. That processing happens locally and the results stay in your browser's extension storage:

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Page addresses and page contentTo apply blocking and hiding rules for the site you're onNowhere. Processed in memory only.
Clicks and key presses (timing only)To tell your real clicks apart from pop-ups and hijacked linksNowhere. Not logged or stored.
Settings, allowlisted sites, your custom filters, elements you blockedTo remember your choicesStored locally in your browser
Blocking statistics (counts per category and per day)To show the dashboard and badge countsStored locally. No URLs are recorded.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No accounts, and no servers operated by us.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, and no advertising.
  • No remote code. All scripts and filter lists ship inside the extension.

The extension makes no network requests of its own. Its blocking rules are compiled from public filter lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Fanboy's lists, uBlock Origin filters and Peter Lowe's list) at build time and bundled with each release.

Your control

You can view, export or delete your settings and statistics at any time from the PowerBlock dashboard (Backup & reset). Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from your browser.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from browser APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Any change to how data is handled will also be announced in the extension's release notes before it takes effect.
